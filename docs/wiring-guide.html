Step by step

Setting up a fresh Seed

The Mac side is already set up (toolchain, dfu-util, ~/dev/DaisyExamples). A new Seed only needs the Daisy bootloader and then ApcBox. Plug in only the Seed you're flashing: the script takes the first Daisy it finds.

  1. Unpack v12

    Unzip ApcBox-v12-sets.zip into ~/dev/DaisyExamples/seed/ so it replaces the ApcBox folder. Your tools/ folder stays. The zip has a ready-built build/ApcBox.bin. To rebuild it yourself:

    cd ~/dev/DaisyExamples/seed/ApcBox && make clean && make

  2. Put the Seed in the chip's own USB mode

    USB-C to the Mac. Press and hold BOOT, press RESET, release RESET, then release BOOT. This starts the STM32's built-in loader, there on every Seed out of the box. Check it with dfu-util -l: it should list @Internal Flash. (Not the same as tapping RESET and then pressing BOOT while the LED pulses: that holds the Daisy bootloader open, which a new Seed doesn't have yet.)

  3. Install the Daisy bootloader

    sh ~/dev/DaisyExamples/seed/ApcBox/flash.sh

    The script sees the chip's mode and writes the Daisy bootloader (dsy_bootloader_v6_4-intdfu-2000ms.bin) into internal flash. ApcBox needs it: the firmware runs from the Seed's QSPI flash and the bootloader starts it.

  4. Replug, then flash ApcBox

    Unplug and replug the USB-C. The LED pulses for about 2 seconds while the bootloader waits. Run the same command again:

    sh ~/dev/DaisyExamples/seed/ApcBox/flash.sh

    It waits up to 20 s for the bootloader and writes ApcBox at 0x90040000. An Error 74 after "File downloaded successfully" is normal. If it doesn't catch the pulse, tap RESET and press BOOT while the LED pulses: that holds the bootloader open.

  5. Check it's alive

    LED flashing fast: ApcBox is running, no APC40 on the USB-A socket yet. Slow: APC40 found. Open ui/apcbox-ui.html in Chrome and connect: you should get the log and the "V 7" version line. From now on flash.sh works with no buttons: it sends "B" over the serial port and ApcBox jumps to the bootloader itself. Close the monitor page first, it holds the port.

  6. Card

    A second card: format it FAT32 with an MBR partition map (Disk Utility: MS-DOS (FAT), Master Boot Record) named APCBOX, then run sdprep.py --go. Or move the card over from the first box: sets, kits and samples all live on it.

Both Seeds on the Mac at once: the monitor page asks which port to use, but flash.sh and dfu-util take the first one. Unplug the other Seed before flashing.

Seed pinout

Coloured pins are the ones ApcBox uses. Grey ones are free. Pin 1 is top left, pin 20 bottom left, pin 21 bottom right, pin 40 top right.

Wire this first

Power and ground

The Seed runs from its USB-C (the Mac). A 5 V breadboard supply feeds the screen's backlight and the USB-A socket's VBUS. The APC40 has its own adapter. Every ground meets at one rail.

One flaky ground wire was what made the first box flash and lose its OLED and SD. Use short, solid ground jumpers and check them with a meter first. Never put 5 V on pin 38: it's the Seed's 3.3 V output, not an input.
USB host

APC40 on a USB-A socket

  • A USB-A female breakout. The APC40's normal USB cable plugs into it.
  • D− and D+ go straight to pins 36 and 37. Keep these two wires short and next to each other.
  • VBUS comes from the 5 V supply, not from the Seed. The APC40 still needs its own power adapter.
  • Wrong way round (D− and D+ swapped): the LED stays on fast flash, nothing is found.
SDMMC1, 1-bit mode

microSD

  • Uses the SD card's native mode, not SPI, but a cheap "SPI" module works: its labels just mean different things (MOSI = CMD, MISO = DAT0, SCK = CLK, CS = DAT3).
  • CS has to be tied to 3.3 V so the card stays in SD mode.
  • VCC: plain breakout with no chips on it, 3.3 V (pin 38). A module with a regulator chip on it, 5 V from the supply. If you're not sure, use 3.3 V.
  • If the log says code 3 (no card answering): add 10 kΩ pull-ups from CMD and DAT0 to 3.3 V, and shorten the wires.
  • Code 13: the card isn't FAT32. exFAT doesn't work.
  • Hot-swap works: pull it, put it back, the box remounts within about 2 s.
SunFounder 3.5" IPS · ST7796U · XPT2046 touch · SPI1

Screen and touch

The screen has a Raspberry Pi socket. Pin numbers on the left are the Pi header's numbering, as printed on the screen's board. ApcBox uses 11 of the 40: 8 for the picture, 3 more for touch. Touch shares the clock and data-in lines with the picture and has its own chip select, data-out and interrupt wires.

Which header pins

Pin positions from SunFounder's own hardware page for this screen. Touch: on the first touch with no saved calibration, a calibration screen comes up. Tap the three crosses (a fingernail works best). It's saved to the card as apcbox/touch.bin. To redo it later: CALIBRATE TOUCH in the SYSTEM view, or hold the encoder down for 3 seconds.

No screen: from v13.29 the box finds out at power-up (the touch chip does not answer on T_DO) and draws nothing. A screen wired without the three touch wires can't be found that way: hold the encoder down at power-up once and it draws from then on.
Backlight on but the picture black: the panel isn't getting its start-up commands. This board has shift registers behind the header, so the firmware sends 16-bit words by default (log: "screen: 16-bit bus"). For a plain SPI screen, hold the encoder push while tapping RESET to switch to 8-bit (saved on the card). Otherwise check CS, DC and RST. Picture mirrored, upside down, or red and blue swapped: change kMadctl in tft.h. Colours look like a negative: set kInvert to false.
EC11 rotary encoder + back button

Encoder

  • Breakout labels vary: CLK = A, DT = B, SW = push.
  • + goes to 3.3 V, never 5 V. The breakout's pull-ups would put 5 V on the Seed's pins.
  • Turning backwards: swap the A and B wires.
  • Back button: a separate momentary switch, not part of the encoder. One leg to pin 27, the other to ground. No resistor needed. The encoder's own GND goes to the ground rail (pin 40), not pin 27.
  • Turn: change. Push: next view (or the action on the sets screen). Hold push 0.7 s or press back: back.
0.96" SSD1306 · I2C1

OLED

  • With no big screen on the Daisy it is the box's own display: pattern, tempo, the selected track and its steps, the last control touched. From v13.30 a box in its bottom line (TRK, PAT, SMP) says what a turn of the encoder does; a push of the encoder changes it. It also shows the sets list when the sets screen is open.
  • Address 0x3C or 0x3D, the firmware tries both.
  • Log says "no reply": SDA and SCL swapped, or VCC/GND loose. Most modules have pull-ups on board.
Codec on the Seed

Audio out and in

Out: 3.5 mm stereo jack

Line level, for a mixer, powered speakers or an audio interface.

In: live sampling

Line level. A mic needs a preamp first: the Focusrite's line out, or a MAX9814 mic board (its output to pin 16, its VDD to 3.3 V).

The Seed's USB-C also carries the audio to the Mac or iPad as a USB sound card. Nothing to wire for that.

Checks

With a meter, power off

  • Pin 40 to supply GND: 0 Ω. Pin 20 (AGND) to pin 40: should read close to 0 Ω.
  • 3.3 V rail to GND, and 5 V rail to GND: no short.
  • No 5 V wire anywhere near pins 38, 21 or the encoder's +.

Power on, monitor page open

  • OLED found at 0x3C
  • screen: SPI up, and the screen shows the STEPS view
  • No "SD not mounted" line. Kits load, the sets screen (SHIFT + DETAIL VIEW) lists the card's sets
  • Turn the encoder: the view changes. Hold its push: back to STEPS
  • Touch the screen: the calibration crosses come up. After that, tapping a view dot in the top right switches view
  • Plug in the APC40: LED goes to slow flash, the APC40 lights up
